## Welcome!

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at <a href="https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/faculty-and-research/finance/faculty/jinyuan-zhang" target="_blank">UCLA Anderson School of Management</a>. I received my Ph.D. in Finance from INSEAD.

My research studies how technology and policy reshape financial intermediaries—banks and mutual funds—and their consequences for the macroeconomy and households.
